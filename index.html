We run the experiment, then show you what actually happened.

NEXORA Lab publishes hands-on tests of AI tools and automation. We give a real job to a real system, show the transcript, and show where it broke — including the parts that did not work.

Primary results, not summaries

Every piece starts with something we actually ran. If we did not test it, we do not cover it.

Costs stated

What a thing costs to run is part of whether it works. We publish the numbers, including when they are unflattering.

Failures published

A review pipeline that never says "this is bad" is an advertisement. We publish the negative findings too.

How this content is made

Our content is produced with the assistance of AI. At present the visuals are generated programmatically and our videos carry no synthetic narration. If we begin using generated imagery, generated video or synthetic voice, this page will say so before that content is published. Where a presenter appears, that character is entirely fictional and is not a real person. We label AI-generated content on every platform we publish to.

The underlying research, testing and editorial judgement are ours. We verify factual claims against primary sources before publishing, and we correct mistakes in public when we make them.
