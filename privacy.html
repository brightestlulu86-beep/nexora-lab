Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

NEXORA Lab is a content publishing system operated for our own social media accounts. It is not a product offered to the public, and it does not collect data from visitors to this website.

1. Who we are

NEXORA Lab is a brand operated by Mountain Information Technology Consultancy EST, an establishment licensed in the United Arab Emirates. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" refer to that operator.

Contact: brightestlulu86@gmail.com

2. This website

This website consists of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds no third-party scripts, and has no forms. We do not collect, store or process any personal data about visitors to these pages.

Our hosting provider may record standard server access logs, including IP addresses, as a normal part of serving the pages. We do not have access to those logs and do not use them.

3. What our software does

NEXORA Lab operates a private content publishing system. It connects only to social media accounts that we own and control, using the official APIs provided by those platforms. It does not connect to, access, or act on behalf of any other person's account.

Specifically, the system:

  • publishes video and image content to our own accounts on platforms including TikTok, Instagram and YouTube;
  • reads performance statistics for our own posts, such as view counts, watch time and engagement figures;
  • stores those figures in a private database in order to measure which content performs well.

4. Data we hold

  • Access credentials. OAuth tokens issued to us by platforms for our own accounts. These are stored in the operating system keychain or an encrypted secret store on infrastructure we control. They are never written into source code, logs, or this website.
  • Our own content. Scripts, captions, generated media, and publishing records for material we produce.
  • Our own performance statistics. Aggregate figures about our own posts, as returned by platform APIs.

We do not collect, purchase, scrape or store personal data about other platform users. We do not build profiles of individuals. We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Comments and messages

Where the system reads comments or messages sent to our own accounts in order to respond to them, that content remains under the control of the platform it was posted on and is governed by that platform's own privacy policy. We do not retain it beyond what is needed to respond, and we do not use it for any other purpose.

6. AI-generated content

Content published by this system is produced with the assistance of artificial intelligence, including AI-generated images, video and synthetic voice. Where a character or presenter appears in our content, that character is entirely fictional and is not a real person.

We apply the AI disclosure labels required by each platform, and we identify AI-generated content in captions and profile descriptions. We do not use the likeness, voice or identity of any real person.

7. Third-party services

To operate the system we use third-party providers for content generation, publishing and infrastructure. When we send data to those providers it consists of our own content — prompts, scripts and media we have produced. Each provider processes that data under its own terms and privacy policy.

8. Retention

We keep our own content and performance records for as long as they are useful for measuring what works. Access credentials are rotated periodically and revoked when no longer needed.

9. Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data from visitors to this website or from other platform users, there is normally no personal data of yours for us to provide, correct or erase. If you believe we hold personal data relating to you, contact us at the address above and we will respond.

If you wish to remove content you posted on one of our social accounts, such as a comment, you can delete it directly on that platform.

10. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.

This policy describes our actual practice and is written in plain language. It is not legal advice, and it has not been reviewed by a lawyer.
